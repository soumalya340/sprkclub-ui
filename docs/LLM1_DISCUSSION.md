### The 4-Chapter Product Journey:

#### 📖 Chapter 1: Skin-in-the-Game Discovery (Inspired by legends.fun)

• No cheap hype or spam: A creator cannot just ask the community for money with zero commitment.
• Before crowdfunding opens, the creator must put down their own stake / deposit to show true commitment.
• The community only rallies behind proposals where the creator has financial skin in the game.

#### 📖 Chapter 2: The Structured Blueprint (Inspired by Omniagent)

• Instead of a vague, messy pitch deck, the proposal is broken into a structured, transparent roadmap:
• Milestone 1: Prototype / Venue Deposit ($5,000)
• Milestone 2: Production & Equipment ($10,000)
• Milestone 3: Final Execution & Delivery ($15,000)
• Every milestone has concrete, non-negotiable proof criteria agreed upon before a single dollar is raised.

#### 📖 Chapter 3: The Unruggable Treasury (Inspired by MetaDAO)

• When the community funds the DAO, the creator does not get the full pot.
• 100% of the funds are locked inside the DAO smart contract.
• The creator can only withdraw funds one milestone at a time. If the project falls apart halfway through, the remaining treasury remains 100%
safe.

#### 📖 Chapter 4: Proof-Before-Payout on 0G Storage (Inspired by Construct)

• When it's time to claim funding for Milestone 1, the creator must upload real, verifiable evidence (vendor receipts, signed agreements,
photos, delivery logs).
• All proof files are permanently saved to 0G Storage with an immutable cryptographic receipt.
• The outcome:
• If the proof is verified ➔ The milestone tranche unlocks.
• If the creator submits fake receipts or ghosts ➔ The DAO automatically pauses payouts and unlocks full refunds for backers from the
remaining treasury.
